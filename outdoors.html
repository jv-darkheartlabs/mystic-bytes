Outdoors

Fishing, coastlines and outdoor interests

Regular recreational fishing in Louisiana’s Delta marshes, familiarity with Gulf Coast species, and time on local waterways.

Fishing, boating and nature observation during personal outings and travel.

Gulf Coast

Recreational fishing in Louisiana’s Delta marshes approximately monthly during 2025–2026. Catches across different outings included speckled trout, flounder, perch, croaker, sheepshead, black drum and redfish.

Time on local waterways includes recreational boating on Lake Pontchartrain and in the Delta marshes. My boat-operating experience was under my grandfather’s supervision during childhood.

Other interests include hiking, nature walks, outdoor photography and visits to parks and wildlife refuges.
