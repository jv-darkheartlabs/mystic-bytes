Slack did not add chat to the office. It became the office. The building is a sidebar. The floor is a channel. Your status is a green dot that means you are available for a tap on the shoulder that never had to walk across a room.

I use Slack as a customer on projects like Nightbind, from a timezone that makes "real-time" a political ask. Salesforce closed the $27.7 billion acquisition in 2021 and named Slack the interface for Customer 360 — a digital headquarters, in their language.[^1] Huddles made the shoulder-tap official: audio without a calendar invite. The incentive is occupancy. If the work is not in the channel, it is not work that the org can see. Presence theater follows: stay green, reply fast, treat away-from-keyboard as a moral event. Interrupt culture is not a personal failing of noisy colleagues. It is the product doing what a chat-shaped office does when the landlord also sells CRM adjacency.

I keep status boring on purpose. Away means away. The office can be a doc. Slack is a vendor that rented the hallway. Walk it on purpose, or it will walk you.

— JV · Dark Heart Labs.

[^1]: Salesforce, "FAQ: Salesforce Completes Slack Acquisition" (21 July 2021); Reuters coverage of the $27.7 billion close. Public ownership and "digital HQ" framing — chat as the workplace, not a sidecar to email.
