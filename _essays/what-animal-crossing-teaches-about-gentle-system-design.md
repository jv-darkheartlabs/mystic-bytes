The island does not fail you for sleeping in. Gentle systems keep people by treating absence as weather, not a broken contract.

I put a "days shipped" counter on an early dark-heart-themes release page. Miss a week of commits and the badge went gold, then grey, then warning red. I called it a nudge. After a stretch of evenings lost to packing, I stopped opening the page. The widget had turned a practice I liked into a debt I owed the UI. Animal Crossing never does this. Skip a day and weeds show up; your standing with the town does not reset to zero. Villagers comment. They do not lock the museum. Soft caps, real-time pacing, and no fail state for a missed session are retention design for people who get sick, travel, and have other jobs.

Streaks belong in games someone opted into. They fail in tools people use to ship work. Return flows should assume a gap and show what changed. Notifications should report, not scold. Progress should still be readable after a week away.

If a daily mechanic would punish illness or a move, cut it. Leave the island standing on Wednesday.
