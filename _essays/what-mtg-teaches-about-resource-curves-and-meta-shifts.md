You lose games on turn three that you thought you were playing on turn six. The curve was a wish. The lands in hand were the budget you actually had.

I did this on a dark-heart-themes plugin pack. A prestige feature shipped before the install path worked on a clean machine. People could not cast the expensive spell because we had skipped the two-drops: docs, a default theme that loaded, an uninstall that did not strand config. Magic's mana curve is sequencing. Play cheap enablers before finishers. Products mirror that. Ship the core loop, then the spectacle. Meta shifts when a new card invalidates last season's deck: a major version, a broken API, a store policy. You adapt or you keep losing with last year's list.

Sideboards exist because you already know the threats. Feature flags and kill switches are the same slot. Greedy decks look brilliant in the goldfish. They die on the draw. A platform policy change is a rotation announcement. Pretending last season's list still casts is how you lose week one.

Count the lands before you add another seven-drop. If the curve does not cast, cut the finisher.

— JV · Dark Heart Labs.
