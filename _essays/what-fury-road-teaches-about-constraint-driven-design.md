If a shot does not move the chase, it is out of the movie. Features should earn screen time the same way. Constraint is not a mood. It is the edit.

I bloated the first mystic-bytes writing index. Filters, series chrome, a second nav, a "featured" rail. The list of essays was still hard to scan. I had spent the sprint on motion around the thing people came for. Fury Road is loud to look at and tight to follow: limited dialogue forced every frame to carry plot. Limited sprint capacity should force the same honesty. They schedule extra syncs when the cut is unclear. The extra meeting is another shot that does not move the vehicle. If you cannot say what the user can do after the release that they could not do before, you added chrome, not propulsion.

Cut until the core loop is obvious: open the index, find a piece, read it. Then, and only then, add a filter that answers a real query. Scope is the edit. Protect it the way an editor protects runtime.

Ship the chase. Leave the filler on the floor.

— JV · Dark Heart Labs.
