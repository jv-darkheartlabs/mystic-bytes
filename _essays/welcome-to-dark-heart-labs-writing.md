This writing shelf is where Dark Heart Labs thinks in public.

You will find technical essays on craft, ops, design, AI, and culture — short claims and longer systems pieces, both in a voice that prefers mechanism over bait. You will find a travel journal: field notes from Louisiana toward Aotearoa, time zones, lodging, and the logistics of doing serious work from a changing desk. You will find wellness notes that treat rest, sensory load, and recovery as infrastructure, with claims kept honest — aromatherapy and recovery included, without miracle language. Occasional film and game pieces borrow from systems thinking when the comparison earns its keep. Projects appear when the writing earns a link to a tier-one repo. The three lanes share a spine. They do not share a costume.

The voice is precise and grounded. Titles describe the claim. Sign-off is JV · Dark Heart Labs. Start anywhere. The [written shelf](/media/written/) splits into [journal](/media/written/journal/) and [literary](/media/written/literary/); essays live here, on their own spine.

No performance of mystery. Just the work, written down — code, movement, and craft, in that order of honesty.

— JV · Dark Heart Labs.
