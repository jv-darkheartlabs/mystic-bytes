About

DarkHeartLabs is a studio of one. Jennifer: senior creative writer, software engineer by training, and the author of a five-volume literary-supernatural series beginning with What the Tide Keeps — the technical work on one side of the desk, the fiction on the other.

For twenty-five years the job has been the same under different weather: make the hard thing readable — documentation, interfaces, systems people have to live inside — and keep the fiction for after hours. The practice is remote, asynchronous, documentation-centered. Accessibility is engineering here: keyboard navigation, practical usability, WCAG standards, not decoration.

The work

Independent creative writer and contractor since 2015; before that, software development, systems administration, and IT support — the years that taught me where systems actually break. The writing covers developer documentation, READMEs, technical guides, incident reports, and outage communications, including plain-language explanations of DNS, email, hosting, deployment, and infrastructure failures, written for the people caught inside them.

The projects are the proof of work; the Journal is the rest of the writing — essays, reviews, poetry, and the films, with every book’s analysis kept on the book’s own page, where it belongs.
