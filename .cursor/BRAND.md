# Brand voice

Two registers. Do not mix them on the same surface.

| Register | Surfaces |
| --- | --- |
| **Employer** | X, LinkedIn, home, CV ledes, outreach |
| **Studio** | About collaborating notes, essays, readings, verse, Discord, gallery |

Studio voice is unchanged this pass. Employer voice is locked from the 2026-08-26 brief.

---

## Employer register (locked)

**Sentence shape.** Recruiter English. Complete sentences. No slang.

**Person.** First person (`I write`).

**Name.** Jennifer Picado. Byline **JV** once if space. Not Dark Heart Labs as the speaker. Legal name on the CV.

**Title.** `Technical Writer` — first line of bios and ledes. Do not use Senior in the label. Seniority lives in the work, the rate, and proof — not the title string.

**Cadence.** One idea per sentence. Title Case for names and headings. Middle dots (`·`) only in a headline stack (`Technical Writer · Accessible Frontend · Remote Technical Support`). No studio lowercase headings, signal-badge voice, or stacked fragments in this register.

**Warmth.** Precise first. One human clause is allowed: `written-first` or `async`. No humor.

**Keywords.** Title plus a few sourcer tokens: documentation, knowledge base, how-tos, incident copy, async, remote, accessibility. Tools (`Jekyll`, `GitHub`, `WCAG`) belong in project lines and the CV, not the bio.

**Evidence.** Artifacts plus one scope line (`platform documentation`, `WCAG 2.1 AA components`). Award names only where a hiring screen expects proof (CV, project write-up). Not in the X bio.

**Availability.** `Open to async remote roles.` Location: `Auckland-bound`. No visa mechanism (AEWV, dates, accreditation) on this register — that stays on About.

**Neurodivergence.** Allowed when it serves the work: documentation for ADHD and other loud minds. Not as identity decoration. Default bios may omit it; a labeled variant may include it.

### Hard bans (this register)

- Founder / LLC / “I run a studio”
- Software-engineer-applicant frame
- Education or certifications unless she names them
- Old brand names (jen-the-dev, ArcaneGlam, and related domains)
- Literary-shelf identity (verse, orchid, dark romance)
- Studio aesthetic: “loud world,” tired-first-time-user metaphor, mission line
- Occupation codes (ANZSCO) outside the CV
- Rates and `$120`
- The word **IC**
