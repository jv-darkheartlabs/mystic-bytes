The listing looked like a design studio. White desk, plant, evening light. On day one at 6pm the upload sat at 8 Mbps while the building streamed dinner. Video calls pixelated. I could download a disk image all afternoon and still fail a standup. Photos are marketing. Throughput at peak local hours is scope.

I ask for a speed test before I book anything longer than a few nights. Not the screenshot from a quiet Tuesday morning. A test at the hour I will actually be on calls, in the room I will sit in, on the network I will use. Hosts who cannot or will not do that are telling me something. In Tāmaki Makaurau I have had fibre that sang and copper that lied. The suburb name on the listing does not settle it.

Desk and chair photos matter as much as the bed. I want to see the work surface, the chair back, the distance to a wall outlet. Kitchen tables wreck wrists by day four. A stool with no lumbar support is a medical bill with extra steps. If the only "office" is a corner of a mattress, I keep looking. Bed is worse than a bad desk. I have tried both.

Cancellation windows need to sit against sprint dates, not against a holiday fantasy. If a review is locked for the 14th, a free-cancel-until-the-13th listing is a tool. A non-refundable month that starts the day after a visa appointment is a trap. Relocation paperwork slips. I have paid to leave a place early because the internet was a rumor and the chair was a dining bench. Flexibility costs more up front. It has been cheaper than a lost week.

Weekly rates are for experiments. Monthly rates are for a desk you have already survived. I treat the first stay as instrumentation: noise at 7am, water pressure, whether the "quiet residential street" hosts a bar. I write down what I need before I upgrade. Upload I can work with. A chair that does not punish me. A door I can close. If those fail, I do not negotiate with my spine. I change rooms.

Short-term housing is a network and a surface. Book the ones that admit measurement. Leave the ones that only admit lifestyle copy.
