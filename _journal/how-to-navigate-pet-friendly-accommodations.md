I do not travel with an animal. Plenty of builders I know do, and the listings lie to them. "Pet-friendly" often means a small dog, a fee you only see at checkout, a ground-floor unit, and a host who will still complain about hair. If a companion animal is non-negotiable, the itinerary shrinks. That is not a lifestyle failure. That is a constraint, and constraints are how you plan.

I treat pet policy the way I treat visa fine print: assume the marketing is incomplete. Read the house rules, not the badge. Write the host before you pay. Ask weight, breed, crate, number of animals, and whether the "friendly" applies to cats, which a surprising number of listings quietly exclude. Screenshot the answers. Deposits can be large. Some are refundable only after a cleaning inspection that takes a week you no longer have.

Airlines are a second policy stack. Cabin versus cargo, temperature embargoes, breed restrictions, health certificates with expiry dates that do not match your ticket change. A missed document does not become a clever story. It becomes a person and an animal stuck in an airport with a refund that will not land this week. I have watched friends rebuild a month around one failed check-in.

Aotearoa is the hard case I actually research, even though I am not flying a pet. Biosecurity here is not a vibe. Import rules, quarantine, and the simple fact that "I'll bring the cat when I relocate" can be a multi-month project. For builders considering Louisiana to Auckland as a real move, the animal is often the dealbreaker that the mood board never mentioned. People delay the relocation. People leave the animal with family. People pick a different country. All of those are honest answers.

If you do not travel with pets, this still belongs in your housing filter. Buildings with a no-pet rule can be quieter. Buildings that allow them may have hallways that smell like a kennel at 7 a.m. when you have a call. Neither is moral. Both affect uptime.

Do not book on the badge. Book on the written rule, the deposit, and the airline's actual PDF. The animal is family to the person bringing it. The listing is not.
