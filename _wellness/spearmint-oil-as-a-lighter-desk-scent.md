Peppermint at a desk can feel like ice cream behind the eyes. Spearmint is the same family with the volume down. I reach for it in the afternoon when I want a cool green note and I do not want a sinus event.

Low-dose diffusion. A drop or two, short session, window cracked. Never near eyes. I skip neat skin use; if it goes on skin it is diluted in a carrier. Pets stay out of the plume. Mint oils can bother cats in particular. Store the bottle away from heat, same as the rest of the shelf.

This is not a stimulant and it is not medical advice. Spearmint may feel fresher than stale office air. It does not replace a walk, water, or the lunch you skipped. Individual response varies. If peppermint already gives you a headache, spearmint is often gentler, but "often" is not a guarantee. Stop if the room starts to feel sharp instead of clear.

I use it as a post-lunch sensory reset, then I turn it off. Living in mint all afternoon is how a light desk scent becomes the thing you blame for the 3pm slump you already had.
