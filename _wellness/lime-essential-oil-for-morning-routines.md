Lime smells like someone cracked a window at 7am. I use it for that cue, not as a personality. One or two drops in a diffuser while the kettle boils. The room gets sharper. I get a start-of-day mark that coffee alone does not always give me.

What I skip is rubbing it on my arms and then walking into sun. Lime is a citrus oil. On skin, then UV, it can be phototoxic. You may get a burn or a dark patch that lasts. Indoor inhalation is the route I trust. If it ever touches skin, it is heavily diluted in a carrier, covered, and nowhere near a sunny commute.

This is not medical advice and it is not a cure for anything. Lime may feel like a morning lift. It does not repay sleep debt. Keep the mist off pets. Cats and small dogs sit lower than your nose and they do not get a vote. Dilute anything that touches skin. Store the bottle away from heat. If you are pregnant, nursing, or managing a condition, ask someone qualified before you experiment.

I crack a window even for a short session. Bright citrus in a closed room turns from lift to headache faster than the calendar thinks.
