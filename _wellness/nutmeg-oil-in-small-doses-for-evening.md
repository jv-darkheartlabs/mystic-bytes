Nutmeg oil is easy to overshoot. The kitchen spice is friendly. The bottle is not. I keep evening use to a whisper of diffusion, then I stop, because more does not mean calmer. It means I misjudged.

I never ingest it. Not in tea, not in a dropper "wellness" shot, not because a forum said a tiny amount was fine. Essential nutmeg and the jar in the cupboard are not the same risk. If you want the pie, bake the pie.

For me it is an evening scent, warm and a little sweet, when I am already winding down. It may help the room feel slower. It does not put you to sleep like a drug, and it does not treat insomnia. If I am wired, I deal with light and screens first.

Dilute if it touches skin, which I usually avoid. Keep pets out of the mist. Store away from heat. This is not medical advice. If you are pregnant, nursing, or on medications, talk to someone qualified. Potency is the whole story here. One drop too many is how a cozy idea becomes a headache and a night you regret.

I would rather underdo it and still like the smell tomorrow than chase a stronger evening and lose the plot.
