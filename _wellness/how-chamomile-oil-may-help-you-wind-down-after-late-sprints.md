After a late deploy, I do not want a sedative in a bottle. I want a cue that the laptop is closed. Chamomile is that cue for me: soft, apple-adjacent, easy to oversell in marketing copy.

If you buy two bottles, know the types. Roman chamomile (Chamaemelum nobile) is the sweeter, more common evening scent. German chamomile (Matricaria recutita) is greener, sometimes blue from chamazulene, and more often talked about for skin. I use Roman in a diffuser after the merge is done, not while I am still reading the incident channel.

The aroma may help some people feel the day is over. It does not treat insomnia. It does not replace a clinician if sleep has been broken for weeks. Pair it with the boring shutdown: lights down, notifications off, food if you skipped dinner for the deploy.

Dilute if it touches skin. Do not ingest it. Keep the bottle away from pets and heat. I skip baths with oil when I am too tired to rinse the tub; residue plus a cat is a bad combination. If you are pregnant, nursing, or managing a condition, ask someone qualified. This is a wind-down note, not a prescription.
