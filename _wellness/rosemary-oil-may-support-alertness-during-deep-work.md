I have read the rosemary-and-memory lore. What I actually notice is a pine-herb room and slightly sharper attention for a short block. That is enough. I do not need a paper to sell me a personality.

Short diffusion. Fifteen or twenty minutes at the start of deep work, then off. If I leave it running I stop smelling it and I start getting a dull head. The folklore is long. The evidence that aroma upgrades cognition is mixed and small. I treat the scent as a cue that this block has a start. Sleep, food, and a closed chat window still do the real work.

I prefer inhalation to skin. If it goes on skin, it is diluted in a carrier and kept away from eyes. Pets do not sit in the plume. Rosemary can feel medicinal in a tight room, so I crack a window. High amounts irritate. I keep it away from infants and anyone who already hates camphor notes.

This is not medical advice. Rosemary oil may support a sense of alertness for some people. It does not cure brain fog. Pregnant or managing a condition: ask a practitioner. If a headache shows up, the experiment is over. Water and air beat another drop.
